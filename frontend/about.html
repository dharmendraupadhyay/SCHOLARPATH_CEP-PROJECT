Our Story

About ScholarPath

We built ScholarPath because we believe every deserving student in India should have equal access to educational funding — regardless of their background, location, or awareness.

🎯

Our Mission

To eliminate the information gap between scholarship providers and eligible students. Thousands of scholarships go unclaimed every year simply because students don't know they exist. ScholarPath changes that.

🌟

Our Vision

A future where no meritorious student drops out of education due to financial constraints. We envision a comprehensive, AI-powered scholarship discovery platform covering every state, category, and level.

Transparency

How ScholarPath Works

🗄️

Curated Database

Our team manually curates and verifies each scholarship — checking eligibility, deadlines, and official apply links before adding to our database.

⚙️

Smart Filtering

Our backend filters scholarships using 8+ parameters from your profile — education, income, caste, gender, state, stream, and special categories.

🔗

Direct Apply Links

We always link to the official government or organization portal — no middlemen, no fees, no redirects to unverified sites.
